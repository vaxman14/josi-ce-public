# Josi CE Privacy Notice

**Effective date: 14 September 2026**

Josi CE is self-hosted. The person or organization operating an installation is
normally the controller of data in that installation. SOCAL RECEPTIONIST LLC
does not receive installation data merely because the software is running.

## Data Josi can process

Depending on enabled features: account and authentication data; profile and
personalization settings; conversations, tasks, approvals and memory; contacts,
calendars, email and connected-account identifiers; uploaded files, extracted
text, search indexes and citations; provider credentials encrypted with the
installation master key; usage/cost records; audit events; backups and recovery
copies; channel identifiers and messages; diagnostics and support material; and
Voice Box audio streamed for local transcription/synthesis.

Family BETA additionally processes the parent-child relationship, controls,
schedules, distinct minutes in which a child sends a message, parental access
events, and child conversations viewed by the linked adult. It is not device or
screen-time monitoring. Ending a relationship removes its schedule, controls
and activity-minute records; ordinary conversation retention remains governed
by the installation.

## Purposes and legal responsibility

Data is used to authenticate users, provide requested assistant functions,
enforce policy and approvals, connect services, secure and operate the
installation, calculate configured usage limits, back up and restore data, and
produce user-approved diagnostics. The operator—not the publisher—selects the
lawful basis, notices, retention and users for a self-hosted installation.

## Who receives data

Data stays on the installation except when a user or operator enables or uses a
destination. Necessary data may then go to: the selected AI provider; Google or
Microsoft for connected accounts; configured SMTP/mail services; Telegram,
Slack, WhatsApp or Signal for external messaging; configured storage/backup
providers; configured GitHub, Netlify, Vercel, Supabase or other developer
services; an operator-selected telemetry or support gateway; recipients and
shared-workspace users authorized by the user; and public documentation/Groq
only when the optional help-chat feature is enabled and a question is asked.
Voice Box is intended to run locally. Legal disclosures may occur when required
by law or needed to protect rights, safety or security. A business transfer may
include data subject to applicable notice and law.

SOCAL RECEPTIONIST LLC does not sell installation data, use it for advertising,
or use private user content to train generalized AI models. Third-party
providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

## Telemetry and support

Telemetry is off by default and requires affirmative opt-in. Its allowlist
excludes prompts, message content, contacts, calendars, credentials and
identifiable business data. Support bundles are created locally for review and
are not sent until a user explicitly approves submission to a configured
gateway. Community installations ship without such a gateway.

## Retention, deletion and rights

Retention is controlled by the operator and feature settings. Users can delete
or export supported content; third-party copies and backups may follow separate
retention. Contact the installation operator to exercise access, correction,
deletion, restriction, objection or portability rights. Publisher questions
follow the public [`SUPPORT.md`](../SUPPORT.md) and [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md)
channels.

## Children

Josi is not a substitute for parental supervision. The operator and supervising
adult must obtain required consent, provide age-appropriate notice, and comply
with child-privacy law. Family BETA visibly tells a managed child who supervises
the account, what can be seen, and when parental access occurred.

## Security and international transfers

Josi uses access controls, encryption for stored provider secrets, audit events,
CSRF protection and restrictive browser policies, but no system is perfectly
secure. Operators control hosting location. Enabling an external provider may
transfer data to countries where that provider operates.
